# Clipboard sharing

Pairwise can move plain text between Macs in the same call. The Clipboard button in the shared-screen toolbar offers two modes:

- **Send clipboard** sends the current text to one teammate. The recipient reviews the offer and chooses **Paste on this Mac**.
- **Sync text clipboard during this call** is opt-in on each Mac. Once enabled, copying plain text with `Cmd+C` shares it with the other call participants, so `Cmd+V` works on their Mac. Each Mac must enable sync. It turns off when the call ends.

Images and files are not transferred. Text is limited to 64 KiB per clip. LiveKit delivers reliable data in packets of at most 15 KiB, so a clip is sent as up to twelve parts (6 KiB of text each) that share an id, and the receiver reassembles them in memory; incomplete clips are dropped after two minutes. Parts go only to current call participants over the call's reliable data channel and are accepted only by their intended recipient. Sync reads the system text clipboard only while its toggle is on; it does not run in the background or outside a call. Turning sync off stops the local clipboard watcher immediately. If only one person enables sync, received text is presented as a reviewable paste offer instead of being written automatically.

Sync never shares pasteboard items that their app marked private: `org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType` (password managers such as 1Password and Bitwarden), `org.nspasteboard.TransientType` (clipboard tools restoring content) and `org.nspasteboard.AutoGeneratedType` (scripted writes). Those copies are skipped with a note in the menu bar panel. While sync is on, the in-call section of the menu bar panel shows a clipboard line with the latest outcome and a **Turn off** button, and the viewer's Clipboard button is highlighted.

This is an early implementation. Test with non-sensitive text first. Apps that do not mark sensitive copies as concealed cannot be filtered; do not enable sync when you do not want copied text shared with everyone in the call.
